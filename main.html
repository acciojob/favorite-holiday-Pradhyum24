Nepal Annapurna Base Camp


The Annapurna Sanctuary is a stunning high-altitude glacial basin located north of Pokhara, Nepal. Surrounded by towering peaks of the Annapurna range, this breathtaking destination offers dramatic landscapes, rich biodiversity, and unforgettable trekking experiences. The region combines lush forests, alpine terrain, and panoramic mountain views.

  1. Annapurna Range – A world-famous mountain range ideal for trekking.
  2. Nepal – A beautiful Himalayan country known for adventure tourism.
  3. Pokhara – A scenic lakeside city with stunning mountain views.
  4. Muktinath – A sacred pilgrimage site for spiritual travelers.
  5. Mustang – A remote cultural region with unique traditions.
  • Momos – A delicious dumpling dish loved across Nepal.
  • Dal Bhat – A traditional staple meal of rice and lentils.
  • Thukpa – A warm noodle soup perfect for cold climates.
  • Sel Roti – A sweet rice bread often made during festivals.
  • Gundruk – A fermented leafy green dish with a tangy taste.
